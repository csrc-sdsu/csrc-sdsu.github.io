Turbulence Modeling with Mimetic Operators

The following image gallery illustrates a simulation sequence of fluid flow behavior between parallel plates (channel flow). The simulation was implemented usig fourth order skew-symmetric mimetic operators. The simulation represents the numerical solution of the incompressible Navier-Stokes equations at a Reynolds number of 180. The first image was captured at t=0.08 secs, and the second at t=0.1940 secs.
